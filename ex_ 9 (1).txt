PROCEDURE:
• Open Terminal in Kali Linux.
• Use the fls command to list the files in the mobile forensic image:
fls -r mobile_image.dd
• Identify database files related to SMS (mmssms.db) and contacts (contacts2.db).
• Note the inode numbers of these files.
• Extract the database files using icat:
icat mobile_image.dd <inode_number> > mmssms.db
icat mobile_image.dd <inode_number> > contacts2.db
• Open the extracted files with SQLite Browser or strings command to view the contents:
strings mmssms.db | less
strings contacts2.db | less
• Analyze the extracted data to obtain SMS messages and contact information.
